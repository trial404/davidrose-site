Orders

When you buy a print or a book, I receive your name, delivery address, email address and the details of what you ordered. This is used to fulfil the contract between us — to print, dispatch and correspond about your order. Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR (performance of a contract).

Payment is handled by Stripe. Card details are entered on Stripe's systems and are never transmitted to or stored by this site. Shipping details are passed to the print lab that produces your order, so that it can be delivered to you.

Invoice and order records are kept for the period required by tax law (generally ten years) and are then deleted. Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(c) GDPR (legal obligation).

Newsletter

If you subscribe, your email address is processed by Substack to send the newsletter and to keep a record of your consent. You can unsubscribe at any time from the link in every email. Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR (consent).

Ebook purchases are processed by Gumroad under their own privacy terms.

Server logs and fonts

The site is hosted on Cloudflare, which processes connection data such as IP address, browser type and timestamps in order to serve the pages and defend against abuse. Those logs are retained for a short period and then deleted. Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR (legitimate interests in a secure and functioning website).

This site currently loads its two typefaces from Google Fonts, which means your IP address is transmitted to Google when a page loads. If you are in the EU and this concerns you, it is worth noting: those fonts can be self-hosted instead, and the stylesheet is already written for that — it is a one-line change documented in the site's README.

This site sets no cookies of its own. Stripe and Gumroad may set cookies that are strictly necessary to process a payment.
