Biography The longer version

I started with a camera and no plan, spent several years photographing the same range of mountains in different weather, and discovered that the interesting part was never the summit — it was the waiting. That turned into the general shape of everything since: long attention, small observations, and a stubborn preference for doing things by hand where handwork still produces something better.

The writing came out of the same instinct. When generative tools arrived, the question that interested me wasn't what they could do but what we'd stop doing. Three books later I'm still circling it.

The builds came out of an engineering background — mostly the habit of describing a thing precisely enough that it can be made, then making it. This website is the most complete example.

Prints and photobooks are made to order in the EU and shipped worldwide. Commissions, exhibitions and licensing are welcome.
