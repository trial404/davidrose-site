================================================================================
 WRITING
================================================================================

 Everything you write for the site lives in this one file. Edit any text
 between the block markers and leave the rest alone.

 HOW IT WORKS
 ------------
 Each block looks like this:

     ### photography.prints.lede
     WHERE: Photography page — Prints section, the lede paragraph
     <blank line>
     The text itself, which can run over several lines.

   `###`   the block's name. DO NOT CHANGE IT. The site looks text up by this
           name; rename it and the text disappears from the page.
   `WHERE` a note saying where this text appears. For you only — it never
           reaches the website. Edit or delete it freely, but keep it on one
           line.
   body    everything after that, until the next `###`. Blank lines are fine.
           Some blocks contain bits of HTML (a <div>, a <span>) — that is the
           layout of that block. Leave the tags, change the words inside them.

 HOW TO PUBLISH YOUR CHANGES
 ---------------------------
     python3 tools/build-copy.py

 That rewrites content/copy.js and content/photos.js, which is what the site
 actually loads. To check whether you have forgotten to run it:

     python3 tools/build-copy.py --check

 RULES OF THUMB
 --------------
   · Never edit content/copy.js or content/photos.js — they are generated and
     will be overwritten.
   · Placeholder text looks like [this]. Search for "[" to find what is left
     to write.
   · Photograph captions are at the very bottom of this file.

 CONTENTS
 --------
   PART ONE — page copy, in reading order
   PART TWO — photograph captions

### photography.01-photography.lede
WHERE: Photography page — 01 Photography section, the lede paragraph

Photography is a way of noticing. What makes a place feel alive —
          light that lasts ten seconds, a room someone has just left, weather
          rearranging a valley. Less a description of the world than a record of
          standing in it and paying attention. Almost all of it is made on foot
          and mostly waiting. The pictures are what's left of the looking.

FAQ ITEMS

### faq.0.q
WHERE: FAQ items (used on the photography page) — item 1, the question

What paper do you print on?

### faq.0.a
WHERE: FAQ items (used on the photography page) — item 1, the answer

Hahnemühle Photo Rag, 308gsm, matte — a cotton rag with a fine texture and no surface glare. The water series is printed on Photo Rag Baryta, which holds more depth in the blacks.

### faq.1.q
WHERE: FAQ items (used on the photography page) — item 2, the question

How long until my print arrives?

### faq.1.a
WHERE: FAQ items (used on the photography page) — item 2, the answer

Prints are made to order, not held in stock. Production takes 3–5 working days. Shipping is then 2–4 days within the EU and 5–12 days elsewhere. You get a tracking number as soon as the parcel leaves the lab.

### faq.2.q
WHERE: FAQ items (used on the photography page) — item 3, the question

Do you ship outside the EU?

### faq.2.a
WHERE: FAQ items (used on the photography page) — item 3, the answer

Yes, worldwide and tracked. Orders leaving the EU may attract import duty or VAT on arrival. That is charged by your customs authority and is not included in the price.

### faq.3.q
WHERE: FAQ items (used on the photography page) — item 4, the question

Can I return a print?

### faq.3.a
WHERE: FAQ items (used on the photography page) — item 4, the answer

Yes. Under EU consumer law you have 14 days from delivery to withdraw from the purchase. Email me, return the print in its original packaging, and I refund the price once it arrives — return shipping is at your cost. If a print arrives damaged, send a photograph and I reprint it at no charge.

### faq.4.q
WHERE: FAQ items (used on the photography page) — item 5, the question

Do you sell framed prints?

### faq.4.a
WHERE: FAQ items (used on the photography page) — item 5, the answer

Not yet. Framing is where most transit damage and cost happens, so I would rather you chose a local framer. Email me and I will happily advise on moulding and mount width for a particular print.

### faq.5.q
WHERE: FAQ items (used on the photography page) — item 6, the question

Will the colours match my screen?

### faq.5.a
WHERE: FAQ items (used on the photography page) — item 6, the answer

Closely, not exactly. Screens vary, and the print is made from an sRGB-profiled file on archival paper. If a print looks meaningfully different from the listing, tell me and I will reprint it.
