How Method

Describe it precisely enough that it could exist.

Build the smallest version that runs. Look at it, decide what's wrong, repeat. Most of the work is the deciding, which is why the notes matter more than the prompts.

The through-line is local-first. Every tool here runs on the machine beside me — no accounts, no uploads, no API bills, and no audio or text leaving the room. Nothing is hosted and nothing phones home.

This website is the simplest example: hand-written HTML, CSS and vanilla JavaScript, a 12-column grid, four colour-coded worlds, no framework, no build step.
